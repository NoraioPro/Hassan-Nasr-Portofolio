    Next steps to use real data

    This prototype computes everything in the browser from a simple model. A real navigator would replace the model with computed positions and real terrain, then keep the same comparison views.

    • Sun and Earth positions: JPL Horizons ephemerides for each site's coordinates, sampled hourly over the chosen dates, with the observer set to the surface site.
    • Local horizon: a terrain mask per site from LRO LOLA topography (or a higher-resolution LRO product for the candidate rim or massif), instead of the synthetic mask here.
    • Site list: the candidate coordinates and names from the mission team's current south-pole landing-site study.
    • Communication: the actual relay or direct-to-Earth coverage schedule from the mission communications plan, in place of the Earth-in-view proxy.
    • Power: a sunlit fraction from the ephemeris and mask, then a lander power model for the chosen hardware.
