Controls

Phone. Drag to scroll (flick to glide), pinch to zoom. Tap a unit to select it, then tap the ground to attack-move, tap an enemy to focus it, tap crystals to mine. Press and hold, then drag, to select a group. Double-tap a unit to select all of that type on screen.

Squads. Press and hold 1, 2 or 3 to save your selection. Tap to recall it, tap again to jump to it.

Context. Workers tapped onto an unfinished building help build it. Vanguard Riggers tapped onto damaged buildings or machines repair them. Move makes units walk without stopping to fight, which is how you retreat.

Computer. Left-drag to select, right-click to command, wheel to zoom, arrow keys to scroll. A attack, M move, S stop, H hold, B build, G mine, R repair, E ability, Ctrl+1–3 save squad, 1–3 recall, Space jump to the last alert.
